Train your pads

Press Record on a pad and make that sound. Every hit is saved as a take, up to 12. A hit fires the pad whose take it matches most closely, so vary the sound a little between takes.

Notes
  • One note at a time. Chords are not supported.
  • Latency is one analysis frame plus the browser audio path, tens of milliseconds.
  • Web MIDI works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Safari has no MIDI output; the built-in synth and MIDI file download still work.
  • Pitch bend range is plus or minus 2 semitones. Set the same range on the receiving synth.
  • Drums send on channel 10: kick 36, snare 38, hat 42.
