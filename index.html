How VOXMIDI works

  • Sing, hum, or whistle into your mic. VOXMIDI tracks the pitch 80 times a second and plays it as MIDI notes.
  • Stay in key snaps your notes to the scale you pick, so even rough humming lands on real notes.
  • Pitch bend: Follow my voice glides between notes like your voice does. Off snaps cleanly to each note.
  • Beatbox a kick, snare, or hat. If it misfires, train the pads to your mouth sounds.
  • MIDI output: pick a device to drive a DAW or hardware synth (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox). Otherwise the built-in synth plays.
  • Record captures everything, then Download MIDI gives you a standard .mid file for your DAW.
  • Panic kills all sound instantly if a note sticks.
